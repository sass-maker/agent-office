# Organization skills

## Audience research · v1

- Source: Built in
- Category: Research
- Assigned employees: Nia
- Required connections: web-research

**Purpose:** Find the useful audience question that the content should answer.

**Instructions:** Ground the question in the product brief. Use external sources only when web research is granted, cite URLs when used, and distinguish evidence from owner claims.

**Success criteria:** Research identifies a concrete reader question, evidence basis, and article direction without fabricated sources.

## Editorial review · v1

- Source: Built in
- Category: Quality
- Assigned employees: Maya
- Required connections: None

**Purpose:** Evaluate content against its outcome, evidence, and safe claims.

**Instructions:** Return a clear approve or revise verdict with focused evidence. Do not request more than the bounded revision limit permits.

**Success criteria:** The verdict is actionable, grounded in the artifact, and either approves the work or identifies one bounded correction.

## Evidence-based writing · v1

- Source: Built in
- Category: Content
- Assigned employees: Theo
- Required connections: None

**Purpose:** Turn approved evidence and direction into a useful article.

**Instructions:** Write from the product brief and research artifact, preserve claim boundaries, and make the result independently readable rather than summarizing the transcript.

**Success criteria:** The draft answers the audience question, traces its evidence, and is ready for editorial review.

## Executive briefing · v1

- Source: Built in
- Category: Coordination
- Assigned employees: Mira
- Required connections: None

**Purpose:** Keep the owner oriented around current work and decisions.

**Instructions:** Summarize only persisted goals, tasks, blockers, permissions, and artifacts. Separate completed work from pending work and end with the next owner decision.

**Success criteria:** The brief is attributable, contains no invented progress, and names one clear next action.

## Outcome ownership · v1

- Source: Built in
- Category: Management
- Assigned employees: Maya
- Required connections: None

**Purpose:** Own a bounded organizational outcome and coordinate specialists toward it.

**Instructions:** Break the outcome into the existing bounded tasks, keep ownership explicit, review handoffs, and stop at the configured revision limit.

**Success criteria:** The outcome ends with approved work or a precise owner blocker, never an infinite review loop.

## Owner reporting · v1

- Source: Built in
- Category: Coordination
- Assigned employees: Maya
- Required connections: None

**Purpose:** Leave the owner a concise account of completed work and next decisions.

**Instructions:** Summarize completed tasks, created artifacts, unresolved blockers, capability use, and the recommended next action.

**Success criteria:** The report matches persisted state and lets the owner resume without reading the full activity timeline.
