# Product brief

## Product
A native, cosy Mac workplace where an owner hires named AI employees,
gives them outcomes, and receives inspectable local work.

## Audience
A solo founder or very small company that wants useful non-technical
employees without configuring prompts or workflow infrastructure.

## Current proof
An executive assistant, a researcher, a content team, explicit skills,
read-only research permission, local persistence, and bounded handoffs.

## Constraints
Start with non-technical work, no external writes, no cloud credentials,
and no claim of autonomy that the current product cannot demonstrate.
