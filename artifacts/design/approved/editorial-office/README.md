# Editorial Office — approved application references

Approved by the owner on 2026-08-10. These five images are the implementation
contract for the native Mac application. They are visual references rather
than raster screens to trace: functional text, controls, focus, state, and
accessibility remain native SwiftUI and SpriteKit.

1. `onboarding.png` — split first-run setup with a warm paper form and an
   illustrated office preview.
2. `office-employee-card.png` — the living Office with a full-height black
   sidebar and a dismissible employee folio floating over the scene.
3. `mission-list.png` — a dense, list-first Mission workspace with grouped
   tasks and a contextual task inspector.
4. `company-members.png` — the Company members wall and relationship view.
5. `employee-details.png` — a durable employee profile containing identity,
   responsibilities, relationships, work, skills, artifacts, and activity.

Shared rules: no global top or bottom bar; warm bone and soft-grey working
surfaces; black reserved for the sidebar, type, structure, portraits, and
employee clothing; expressive people; restrained native controls; no generic
dashboard card grid.
