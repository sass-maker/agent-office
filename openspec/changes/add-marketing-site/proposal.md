## Why

The native POC now has a distinctive product experience, but no public surface
that explains what it is, lets someone see the real workplace, or makes the
commercial model understandable. The landing page should make today's product
legible without inflating it into a future platform.

## What Changes

- Add a dependency-free, responsive marketing site that inherits the app's
  handcrafted golden-hour workplace.
- Lead with the real Mac application and one concrete content-team workday.
- State the commercial model clearly: the Mac workplace is a one-time
  purchase, while employable AI employees may be optional subscriptions.
- Introduce Maya, Nia, and Theo as the first demonstrable team without claiming
  that an employee marketplace or production purchase flow already exists.
- Include accessible navigation, reduced-motion behavior, and useful mobile,
  tablet, and desktop compositions.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `marketing-site`: A persuasive public page that demonstrates the current Mac
  product, explains ownership and employee subscriptions, and offers honest
  actions appropriate to the POC.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

- Adds static files under `site/` and marketing-specific imagery under
  `site/assets/`.
- Updates `PRODUCT.md`, `DESIGN.md`, the design-review receipt, and durable
  project status.
- Adds no production dependency, backend, payment system, analytics, deploy,
  or public release.
