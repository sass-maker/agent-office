## Purpose

Provide an honest, persuasive public explanation of the Mac workplace, its
first working employee team, and its ownership-plus-subscription business model.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Visitor understands the current product
The site SHALL identify the product as a native Mac workplace where named AI
employees coordinate work, and SHALL distinguish current POC behavior from
future capabilities.

#### Scenario: First viewport
- **WHEN** a first-time visitor opens the landing page
- **THEN** they can identify the Mac application, the employee concept, and a
  clear next action without scrolling

### Requirement: Commercial model is explicit
The site SHALL explain that the workplace application is intended as a one-time
purchase and that optional employees can be separate recurring subscriptions,
without inventing prices or claiming purchasing is currently available.

#### Scenario: Visitor evaluates ownership
- **WHEN** a visitor reaches the ownership section
- **THEN** the workplace purchase and optional employee subscription are shown
  as separate concepts in plain language

### Requirement: Real product evidence leads
The site SHALL use the real application artwork and the first content-team
workflow as its primary proof instead of fabricated customer claims, metrics,
or integrations.

#### Scenario: Visitor inspects the workday
- **WHEN** a visitor explores the product demonstration
- **THEN** they see Maya, Nia, and Theo move research through writing, review,
  revision, and an owner report

### Requirement: Page remains accessible and responsive
The site SHALL preserve readable content, working navigation, keyboard focus,
and reduced-motion behavior across phone, tablet, and desktop layouts.

#### Scenario: Constrained visitor experience
- **WHEN** a visitor uses a narrow viewport, keyboard navigation, or reduced
  motion
- **THEN** all essential content and actions remain available without relying
  on the decorative animation

### Requirement: Calls to action are honest
The site SHALL offer actions that work in the local POC context and SHALL NOT
present an unavailable payment or employee-hiring flow as live.

#### Scenario: Visitor follows a primary action
- **WHEN** a visitor activates the primary call to action
- **THEN** the page moves to the real product demonstration or local run
  instructions rather than a fake checkout
